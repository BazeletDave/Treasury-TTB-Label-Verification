Design Decisions

  • Deterministic compliance checks for fields where exactness matters.
  • Normalization for harmless formatting differences such as capitalization and punctuation.
  • Human review status for ambiguous or near match cases.
  • No storage of uploaded images or label text in this prototype.
  • Architecture leaves OCR or vision integration as a replaceable extraction layer.
Prototype for evaluation only. Not connected to COLA or Treasury production systems.
